# True Peak Plans & Pricing

True Peak helps music labels and DJs filter and screen track demos automatically using DSP audio analysis. Find the plan that best fits your workflow.

---

## 1. Free Plan
**Price:** $0 / month  
*Perfect for starting out and testing the platform.*

### Features & Limits:
*   **Monthly Volume:** Up to 10 demos / tracks per month.
*   **Analysis:** Basic technical audio analysis.
*   **Workflow:** Personal submission link and basic dashboard access.
*   **Storage Terms:** Audio files are processed in real-time. HQ original lossless files are not stored long-term (only lightweight streaming previews are preserved).

---

## 2. Indie Plan
**Price:** $29 / month  
*Designed for active indie labels and touring DJs.*

### Features & Limits:
*   **Monthly Volume:** Up to 100 tracks / demos per month.
*   **Analysis:** 
    *   Structural Filters (BPM, LUFS target, Track Duration).
    *   DSP Filters (Stereo Phase Correlation, Digital Clipping/True Peak, Crest Factor/Dynamics).
*   **Automated CRM:** Up to 100 automated emails per month. Save time with dynamic templates (variables auto-fill with track metadata).
*   **Playback:** Lossless Web Player directly in the browser dashboard.
*   **Support:** WhatsApp assistance for quick setup and troubleshooting.
*   **Storage Terms:** Secure hosting and storage of original high-quality lossless files (WAV, FLAC, AIFF) for **7 days**.

---

## 3. Pro Plan
**Price:** $79 / month  
*For professional record labels and busy promotional agencies.*

### Features & Limits:
*   **Monthly Volume:** Up to 500 tracks / demos per month.
*   **Analysis:** 
    *   **Exclusive Harmonic Filter:** Camelot Wheel scale compatibility checks.
    *   All Structural and DSP Filters.
*   **Automated CRM:** Professional CRM access with up to 500 automated emails per month.
*   **Playback:** Lossless Web Player.
*   **Support:** Priority WhatsApp Support with direct access to engineering staff.
*   **Storage Terms:** Secure hosting and storage of original high-quality lossless files (WAV, FLAC, AIFF) for **14 days**.
